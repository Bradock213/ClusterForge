# Code of Conduct

ClusterForge aims to keep technical collaboration respectful, focused and useful.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and assume good intent
- focus criticism on code, behavior and technical decisions rather than people
- provide reproducible details when reporting problems
- respect privacy and never publish another person's credentials or private infrastructure data
- keep discussions relevant to the project
- accept maintainer decisions on scope, security and release readiness

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, doxxing, intentional disruption, credential exposure or repeated hostile behavior are not acceptable in ClusterForge spaces.

## Scope

This applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, code review and other project-managed communication channels.

## Enforcement

Content that violates these expectations may be edited, hidden, locked or removed. Accounts that repeatedly or seriously violate the policy may be blocked from project spaces.

For security-sensitive matters, use the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) rather than a public issue.
